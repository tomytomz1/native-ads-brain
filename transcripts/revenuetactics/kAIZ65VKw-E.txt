Tom Bell from Revenue Tactics. Here's the framework I use and teach. Three to five angles per offer. Each angle gets three to five headlines, real variations, not tiny swaps. And each angle gets two to three creatives that match the emotions of the angle. So, you're not testing random ads. You're testing ideas. Then you let the data tell you what's happening. If one angle wins on CTR, you scale that angle and build more headlines around it. If another angle wins on conversions, you can lean into that story and improve the bridge. This keeps testing controlled, fast, and repeatable instead of guessing.
