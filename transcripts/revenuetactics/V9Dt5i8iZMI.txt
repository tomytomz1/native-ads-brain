Quick gravity breakdown because people misunderstand it. Gravity isn't how many sales a product made. It's more like how many affiliates have recently earned commissions on it weighted by recency. So, high gravity usually means lots of affiliates are promoting it successfully, which can be a really good sign. But, it can also mean the offer is competitive and potentially ad fatigued. The move is to use gravity as a signal, not as a decision-making process. Check the VSL quality, check the funnel, check EPC, then test your own angles cuz even a hot offer needs a fresh emotional hook to stand out.
