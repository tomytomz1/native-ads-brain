The number one reason people lose money on native ads isn't bad traffic. It's that they treat testing like gambling. They launch one ad, one headline, one creator, spend a bit, and then panic and start changing everything. And when you change everything, you can't diagnose anything either. Native ads need structured testing. You test multiple angles, you track CTR and conversion rate separately, and you make changes one lever at a time. If CTR is low, it's your ad. If CTR is good, but no sales, it's your bridge or offer mismatch. If you don't separate those, you'll just keep bleeding budget and blaming the platform.
