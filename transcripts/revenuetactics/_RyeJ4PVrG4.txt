The metric beginners ignore is what happens after the click. Everyone stares at CTR and CPC. They don't track, did people actually click through to the offer from my pre-sell? And what was the percentage? What's the traffic intent like at that point in the funnel? That one number tells you if your pre-sell is bridging properly. If CTR is good, but click through to the offer is low, your page is boring, confusing, or mismatched to the ad. So, track the funnel like a pipeline, ad click, pre-sell engagement, offer click, conversion. Once you do that, you stop guessing, you know exactly which lever to pull next.
