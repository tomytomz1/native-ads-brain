Hi, Tom Bell from Revenue Tactics. If your CTR is low, don't immediately blame the offer. CTR is basically your ads curiosity score. And if people aren't clicking, your funnel might be amazing, but you'll never get enough of the right traffic to find out. So, before you switch offers, fix these three things first. Number one, make the promise more specific. Lose weight is vague. Drop 9 lb in a month is clear. Second, build a curiosity gap. Hint at the reason without giving the full answer in the headline. Thirdly, match your creative to the emotion. If the angle is frustration, don't use a smiling stock photo. If it's fear, don't use something that feels playful. Then test three to five angles, not micro headline tweaks. If CTR is dead across angles, then you can question the offer.
