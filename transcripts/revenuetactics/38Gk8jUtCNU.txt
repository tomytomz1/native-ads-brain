If you keep switching ClickBank offers every few days, that's probably why you're not profitable yet. I get it. You launch, you spend a bit, nothing happens, and you assume the offer must be dead. But some of the time, it's not the offer. It's that you never stay with one offer long enough to get clean data. Here's what to do instead. Pick one proven offer and commit to a 7 to 14-day test window. During that window, you can only change four things: the angle, the headlines, the creatives, and the pre-sale, the landing page, into the VSSL. Because offer hopping changes too many variables at once, and then you don't learn anything. Stick with one offer, test multiple angles, track everything, and let the data tell you what to do next.
