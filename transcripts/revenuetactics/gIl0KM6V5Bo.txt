If you want to stop guessing, read native data in the right order. First is CTR. That tells you if your ad angle is compelling. Then look at clicks to the offer. That tells you if your pre-sell is doing its job as a bridge. Then look at conversion rate. That tells you if the offer and message match the audience. Finally, EPC tells you what the traffic is worth overall. Most beginners jump straight to did I make a sale? Pros diagnose the funnel like a pipeline, ad, bridge, offer.
