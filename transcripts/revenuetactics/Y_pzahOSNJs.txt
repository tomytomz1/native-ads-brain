If your campaign dies after day two, don't panic. A lot of the time you just hit the first wave of easy placements and then performance drops when you move into broader inventory. Here's what you do. First, don't touch everything. Check CTR. Did it drop? If yes, refresh creatives and headlines. If CTR is stable but conversions drop, your traffic quality shifted. Tighten targeting placements or pre-sell message. Also look for ad fatigue signs. Same creative, same headline, same story for too long.
