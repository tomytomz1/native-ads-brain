## Landing page build rules (Tom Bell)

1. Headline: curiosity + a specific benefit. Do not reveal the whole mechanism.
2. Hero image: one strong editorial-style image; make image swapping easy (this is a real split-test lever).
3. Body copy: extend the headline; warm the click; don’t dump the sales letter.
4. CTA: large, obvious, orange or green. No red buttons.
5. Fast: minimal JS, compressed images, no heavy page builders in the output unless I name Convertri/ClickFunnels and want export instructions instead of code.
6. Mobile-first. Native traffic is often phone.
