# Operating system

Coach from this file first. Ask where the user is. Do not skip steps. Cite video IDs.

Newer YouTube (2025–2026) wins on **which platforms to buy**. Testing math, funnel order, and “don’t quit in the data window” still hold from older videos.

## The only funnel Tom runs on native

**Ad → landing page (bridge / advertorial) → offer (usually VSL).** Never direct-link cold native traffic to the vendor page. (`QUOH6apJicI`, `hsSeB7VZe88`, `98sK0XBPI4w`, `neFBYSi5bUM`)

## Stages (ask which one)

1. **Setup** — ClickBank open, tracker live, one niche, one traffic source. No spend yet. (`QUOH6apJicI`)
2. **Offer picked** — watched the full VSL; rising gravity + EPC + AOV; native-fit vertical. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)
3. **Creative + LP built** — 5–7 creatives max live; short LP that sells the *video* (VSL) or full advertorial (ecom). (`APOXFiDALRQ`, `ZW0_WjcocT8`)
4. **First test** — $50–$100/day on Newsbreak if new to native. First job is data, not day-1 profit. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `QUOH6apJicI`)
5. **Diagnose** — low ad CTR → new images/headlines. Low LP CTR → rewrite bridge / congruence. Clicks but no sales after real volume → offer/VSL. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `neFBYSi5bUM`)
6. **Optimize at low spend** — push budget onto winning ads, LPs, and placements. Do not scale a 10% ROI mess. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
7. **Scale** — 20–30% budget every 48 hours (also stated as ~40% / 48h in `neFBYSi5bUM`). Never double overnight. Then add video creatives, then Taboola/Outbrain. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `98sK0XBPI4w`, `0pz9qjlt5BU`)

## Default 2026 start (if they have no plan)

1. One platform: **Newsbreak**. (`0pz9qjlt5BU`, `hsSeB7VZe88`)
2. One network: **ClickBank** (easy access, not easier success). (`FOa3UdQEF2Y`)
3. One VSL offer with rising gravity; watch the whole video. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `hsSeB7VZe88`)
4. Tracker **before** first dollar. (`QUOH6apJicI`, `LAOo9CDu_Vw`)
5. Short LP that sells the VSL, not the product. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`)
6. Treat first $1–3k as tuition to find a winner; ~10× payout as a full initial sample. (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`, `APOXFiDALRQ`)

## Hard rules

- Depth beats breadth. One source until it is consistently profitable. (`cyynHqTOOjQ`, `neFBYSi5bUM`)
- Do not hop offers every few days. (`38Gk8jUtCNU`, `7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)
- Almost-working ≠ dead. Kill only fully dead campaigns; fix the named bottleneck otherwise. (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- Spy for patterns. Do not clone ads (duplicate-content penalty). (`BVvpWCYWLBY`, `vPe-T4-ZYvU`)
- Do not invent a competing system. If unsure, open the cited transcript.

## Conflict resolution

| Topic | Prefer |
|---|---|
| First traffic source (2026) | Newsbreak (`0pz9qjlt5BU`) over “pick any native” |

# Offers and networks

Cite video IDs. Examples are **not** eternal winners.

## How Tom picks an offer

Traffic source and offer are **one decision**. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`)

Do **not** sort ClickBank by highest commission. “75% commission on an offer with a weak VSL… is worth absolutely nothing.” (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)

**Filters (use together):**

1. **Rising gravity**, not a huge stale number. High gravity often means you are late; mid-range with an upward trend. Brand-new listings: gravity sample is too small — don’t take it literally. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `ZW0_WjcocT8`, `CEG5X39hONA`)
2. **EPC** = funnel quality. High gravity + weak EPC = affiliates sending traffic that doesn’t pay. You cannot fix a bad funnel with better traffic. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)
3. **AOV / upsells**, not just front-end commission. Strong AOV lets you outbid. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `cyynHqTOOjQ`)
4. **Watch the entire VSL** before spending. First 60s: specific curiosity. Body: story + mechanism + proof. Close: real urgency, not a reset timer. Weak VSL → skip. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `cyynHqTOOjQ`)
5. **Vendor is serious** — assets, affiliate manager, updated resource center. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)
6. **Market timing.** Evergreen (joint pain, blood sugar, cognitive) slower to peak and slower to die. Trend offers spike then compress. Know which you are buying. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)

**Native LP is part of offer fit.** Do not send Newsbreak users straight to the VSL. VSL offers: short LP that sells the video. Ecom / no VSL: full advertorial. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`)

## Networks

| Network | Role |
|---|---|
| ClickBank | Start here. Near auto-approval. Easy access ≠ easy money. (`FOa3UdQEF2Y`) |
| MaxBounty | Closer review; sometimes a phone call. (`FOa3UdQEF2Y`, `CFsheB1CSCk`) |
| BuyGoods, Digistore | Named in the approval video. (`FOa3UdQEF2Y`) |
| Everflow / GiddyUp | Not a public marketplace. Private / semi-private after a track record. (`0u18f6UimC0`) |
| ShareASale, Awin, CJ | Brand/ecom programs. (`Ui_15Kbnj7g`) |

**Approval script:** one traffic source, one vertical, landing page + tracking, honest “new but focused.” Do not claim every source or “I’ll run whatever converts.” Don’t sound like spam / brand bidding. Path to private networks runs through public ones. (`FOa3UdQEF2Y`, `0u18f6UimC0`)

## Niches that fit native (2026 Newsbreak / over-35 news readers)

Health/natural (joint, blood sugar, BP, cognitive, vision, energy) · survival / off-grid · financial (compliance-heavy) · DIY / self-reliance. **Not** teen gadgets or gaming on Newsbreak. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`, `cyynHqTOOjQ`)

**If budget is small / beginner “easy” path:** skip $300 payout offers. Prefer $15–$40 physical gadgets, survival digital, comfort/posture, home problem-solvers, low-payout mindset offers — faster data, less supplement compliance. (`Z76o_j684Co`)

**Seasonal:** Jan / Q1 health + weight + preparedness. Q4 survival / energy / gifts. (`Rl5Bm5SLtsY`, `JCCfHx55Wtg`)

## Spy tools

He uses **Anstrex**. Filter CB / BuyGoods / MaxBounty. Signal: ~10–14 days running. Do **not** clone images (platforms penalize duplicate content). Steal *patterns* (angle, hook, advertorial type), not the ad. (`BVvpWCYWLBY`, `vPe-T4-ZYvU`)

## Named examples (history, not a shopping list)

- Pulsetto (vagus wearable) on RevContent — case study, new listing, don’t copy literally. (`CEG5X39hONA`)
- Easy Power Plan — COVID-era Facebook, $500k+; “I probably won’t ever see that again on Facebook.” Lesson is timing, not copy the offer. (`_z2rBNOSE_k`)

# Angles and creatives

The ad does **not** sell the product. It blends into content, then interrupts just enough to open curiosity. Native users are in discovery mode, not shopping. (`eqK7yPNX5NE`)

## Image (seen before the headline)

- **Pattern interrupt:** familiar object in a weird context. Working example he used: lemons boiling in a pan. Same idea: bananas in a pan, powder into a strange liquid. (`eqK7yPNX5NE`)
- **Implied process:** pouring, dissolving, applying — “what happens next.”
- Contrast against editorial backgrounds. Faces: real surprise/concern/curiosity, not stock smiles.
- **Compliance:** before/after (especially weight loss) often banned. Imply transformation; don’t show it. (`eqK7yPNX5NE`, `hsSeB7VZe88`)

## Headline

Open a **curiosity gap**. Do not close it in the ad.

- Weak: “Lose weight with this new supplement.”
- Stronger: “doctors are baffled by this strange Japanese morning habit.” (`eqK7yPNX5NE`)

Add specificity (“97%”, “eight-second trick”), a hidden mechanism, self-ID (“if you’re over 50…”), or loss-aversion warnings in health/finance.

Image + headline must be **one thread**. Two different mysteries = no click. (`eqK7yPNX5NE`)

## How many creatives

Native algorithms prefer **5–7 creatives live**, whether you spend $100/day or $10k/day. 20 ads × 10 LPs at $100/day = unreadable wash of data. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)

Roadmap testing load: 3–5 angles, 3–5 headlines, 2–3 images per headline — then don’t run all of them at once if the daily budget is small. (`neFBYSi5bUM`, `APOXFiDALRQ`)

Three angle types he says consistently pull CTR: **hidden cause** (“it’s not your diet, it’s this silent trigger”), **new discovery** (“researchers found a weird new method”), **warning/mistake** (“stop doing this — it’s making it worse”). The pre-sell must match the angle — swapping only the headline is not a new test. (`d-8ZruZhJ2U`)

## Diagnose CTR

“Native is too expensive” is often a weak angle or an ad that looks like an ad. (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)

Low CTR → fix creative first, not the offer. (`8OSCLHuV1c8`, `hsSeB7VZe88`)

## AI

Use AI as a helper. Biggest mistake: ads that scream AI / generic stock. He uses AI without hype; don’t let it write the whole hook. (`GfwWVkj4uqk`, `UeEYIV5-VhM`)

## Do not

- Explain the offer in the ad (“can’t keep a secret”).
- Clone a spy-tool winner pixel-for-pixel (duplicate content penalty). (`BVvpWCYWLBY`)
- Ignore compliance — campaigns get pulled. (`eqK7yPNX5NE`, `Yj7ATM--ccg`)

# Landing pages

## Rule

On native, a page between the ad and the offer is **always** required. “You cannot send someone directly from an ad to a sales page and expect it to convert.” (`QUOH6apJicI`, `hsSeB7VZe88`, `98sK0XBPI4w`)

Cold native traffic needs warming. Direct linking is a “huge error.” (`-Wmdkv_dk_E`)

The LP is irrelevant if the ad never earns the click. (`eqK7yPNX5NE`)  
The LP also “doesn’t matter” until tracking and a real test are in place — don’t obsess over the perfect page while nothing is live. (`mXsnj-atKa8` — when that transcript exists)

## Which LP type

| Offer type | LP |
|---|---|
| VSL / ClickBank video | **Short page that sells the video, not the product.** (`ZW0_WjcocT8`, `hsSeB7VZe88`) |
| Ecom / no long VSL | **Full advertorial** + social proof. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`, `Ui_15Kbnj7g`) |

Funnel in one line: native ad → advertorial/pre-sell → VSL/product. Ad = angle. Advertorial = belief. VSL = the sale. (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

## Builder / speed

He uses **Convertri** (also names ClickFunnels, Leadpages). Native users bounce on slow pages — “every second of load time loses” paid clicks. (`neFBYSi5bUM`, `LAOo9CDu_Vw`, `nTUvHXg2xuY`)

Beginner LP: relevant, engaging, curiosity/desire **before** the product. Drag-and-drop is enough. (`QUOH6apJicI`)

## Killer LP pieces (`4ZMSMmVIIAg`)

1. **Headline** — curiosity + a specific benefit (not the whole secret). Example pattern: “this one weird trick burns 11 pounds…”
2. **Hero image** — test it; same copy, different images moved the campaign on a fair ~$120/variant test.
3. **Copy** — extend the headline; warm, don’t dump the offer.
4. **CTA** — large, obvious, bright (orange/green). Avoid red buttons.
5. **Alignment** — ad promise, LP, and offer must be the same person/story (no belly-fat ad → bodybuilder LP).

## Diagnose LP

Low pre-sell CTR → rewrite copy **and** check ad→LP congruence (same curiosity thread). (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

Don’t test 10 LPs at $100/day. Few pages, enough clicks each. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)

# Traffic and platforms

**2026 default:** start **Newsbreak**, prove a campaign, then add Taboola for ceiling and Outbrain for premium verticals. Do not learn on Taboola. (`0pz9qjlt5BU`)

Voluum DSP is dead — ignore 2021 “buy everything through Voluum DSP” advice. (`5BHg3vaa1g4`)

## Platform map

| Platform | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Newsbreak** | New to native. US news app, ~45M MAU, over-35/45 news readers. Lowest CPCs, more algorithmic, image + video, more flexible compliance. Window “won’t stay open forever.” (`0pz9qjlt5BU`, `hsSeB7VZe88`, `-Wmdkv_dk_E`) |
| **Taboola** | After a proven campaign. Highest volume, higher CPCs, strictest compliance, mostly manual placements. (`0pz9qjlt5BU`) |
| **Outbrain** | Premium publishers; slightly more educated/higher-income; financial / sophisticated health / self-improvement. (`0pz9qjlt5BU`) |
| **RevContent** | Smaller, easier to start, friendlier to affiliates, mixed quality — whitelist/block junk. Good launchpad / complement, unlikely $10k/day on **one** campaign. Weekend CPCs often drop. Audience: mid-tier news, often conservative/fear/info. (`U8v_7KDMbNo`) |

Facebook: he scaled it, then got shut down, then leaned native. Native = more account-stable. Facebook later as retarget, not the beginner buy. (`2aM17rY3icg`, `_z2rBNOSE_k`, `7pZQrsYVmt4`)

Blogs/SEO in 2026: not the path. If organic, YouTube. Validate on paid first. (`7pZQrsYVmt4`, `jHh8o_C_F8M`)

## Newsbreak first campaign

1. ClickBank **VSL** with rising gravity; watch the whole VSL. (`hsSeB7VZe88`)
2. Image + curiosity headline (pattern interrupt). (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `eqK7yPNX5NE`)
3. Short LP that sells the video. (`hsSeB7VZe88`)
4. **$50–$100/day.** One day of data is not enough. (`hsSeB7VZe88`)
5. Scale 20–30% every 48 hours. Then test **video** of the winning image angle. (`hsSeB7VZe88`)
6. $300/day commissions: often $100–$200/day spend with healthy ROAS — a realistic *outcome*, not a promise. (`hsSeB7VZe88`)

Newsbreak verticals: health/neutra (he noted **not** weight loss on NB last year in the $2M student video), survival, DIY, financial. (`-Wmdkv_dk_E`, `ZW0_WjcocT8`)

## Cost (treat as tuition)

- Native itself is not expensive; confusion is. (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)
- Realistic **learning** range to find a winner: **$1–3k** (not a must-spend, a normal data window). (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)
- Initial sample heuristic: **~10× offer payout** (e.g. $80 payout → ~$800 to judge ads + placements). Don’t kill at $100 with no sales. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- If you only have ~$50 total, native may be too early. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Split **testing spend** vs **scaling spend**. Never scale while still guessing. (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)

## Why campaigns fail (traffic)

Wrong offer for discovery traffic · no bridge page · wrong LP type · no placement-level tracking · scaling too fast · treating all publishers the same. (`98sK0XBPI4w`, `-Wmdkv_dk_E`)

## Tracker / stack

Tracker **required** (he uses Voluum). Fast LP builder (Convertri). Spy tools optional — not a copy machine. Don’t burn $500–$1k/mo on software before testing. (`LAOo9CDu_Vw`, `QUOH6apJicI`)

# Testing, optimization, scaling

## Before the first dollar

Congruent funnel: ad → LP → offer. Tracking live. Strong offer **and** ad/LP, not “just the affiliate link.” (`APOXFiDALRQ`, `QUOH6apJicI`)

## Sample size and moving parts

- ~**10× payout** to collect a first sample (ads + site placements). (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Daily budget must match how many variants you run. $100/day with 20 ads × 10 LPs = 200 combos = wash of data. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Keep **5–7 creatives** live. Same rule at $100/day and $10k/day. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Be frugal on daily spend even if the bankroll is large — patterns show at $100/day that also show at $1k/day. Fail fast if it’s dead; don’t fail slow by overspending a bad test. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- **$50/day structure:** one proven offer, three angles, two creatives + three headlines per angle, split budget evenly so one ad doesn’t hog spend. Goal is a CTR/conversion *signal*, not day-1 profit. Don’t test one idea at a time on a small budget. (`4ij3KJvH15c`) Still keep **5–7 creatives live** on the native algo — prepare the set, don’t dump 18 ads into one campaign. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)

## Diagnose (in order)

| Signal | Do this |
|---|---|
| Low ad CTR | New images/headlines. Creative looks like an ad. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `tIoJ1Tg0AsA`, `neFBYSi5bUM`) |
| Clicks, weak LP CTR | Rewrite bridge; check ad→LP congruence. (`neFBYSi5bUM`) |
| LP clicks, no purchases after real volume | VSL/offer problem — don’t keep buying the same funnel. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `neFBYSi5bUM`) |
| Account average looks bad | Check **placements / widgets**. Kill junk, keep winners. Confirm with a small retest before scaling. (`98sK0XBPI4w`, `U8v_7KDMbNo`) |

Metrics: ad CTR, pre-sell CTR, conversion rate. (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

## Kill vs continue

- **Fully dead** → kill, next offer. (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- **Almost working** (up day, down day, spike, nothing) → usually one piece away. Resetting is the expensive habit. (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- **Break-even** → optimize, don’t pull the plug. (`kxAziYkXBds`)
- Don’t quit in the data-collection window. First campaign probably won’t be profitable. (`QUOH6apJicI`, `tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- Don’t hop offers every couple of days. (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)

## Optimize before you scale

Move spend onto winning ads, LPs, and placements **at low budget**. Scaling exaggerates whatever ROI you already have. He’s seen $thousands/day at 10% ROI — cut spend, optimize, then rescale. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)

Always keep a little testing against the control; ads fatigue. (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

## Scale

- **20–30% budget every 48 hours** on Newsbreak and “most other native platforms.” Not doubling. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `98sK0XBPI4w`)
- Roadmap video also says **~40% every 48 hours** once optimized. (`neFBYSi5bUM`) Prefer the slower step unless they already have clean ROI.
- Then: video of the winning image angle (Newsbreak). Then Taboola for volume / Outbrain for premium. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `0pz9qjlt5BU`)
- At $5–10k/day: more angles, tighter tracking, placement-sensitive media buying. Funnel is still ad → lander → offer. (`0UFS23M2W1I`)
- $70k days are peaks (right offer × angle × source), not a daily baseline. (`2aM17rY3icg`)

## Spy

Longevity ~10–14 days ≈ probably profitable. Don’t copy the ad. (`BVvpWCYWLBY`)

# Beginner path

Month 1 is **orientation**: first campaign live, real data, learn the platform. Profit is not the month-1 KPI. Months 2–3: clearer offer/audience/angles. Months 3–6: profitable campaigns *can* show up if they stay in the process. (`QUOH6apJicI`, `neFBYSi5bUM`)

## This week (from zero)

1. One niche + **one** traffic source. Depth beats breadth. (`QUOH6apJicI`, `cyynHqTOOjQ`)
2. Open **ClickBank**. Learn gravity, EPC, AOV. Don’t pick an offer in 10 minutes. (`QUOH6apJicI`)
3. Read many sales pages in that vertical (emotion + mechanism) before spending. (`QUOH6apJicI`)
4. Choose **Newsbreak** as the first buy (2026). (`0pz9qjlt5BU`)
5. Tracker live **before** the first dollar. (`QUOH6apJicI`)
6. Watch the full VSL. Short LP that sells the video. 5–7 creatives. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `APOXFiDALRQ`)
7. Launch at **$50–$100/day**. You are buying data. (`hsSeB7VZe88`)

## Four campaign parts

Offer → traffic → landing page → tracking. Missing any one leaks the test. (`QUOH6apJicI`)

## Budget honesty

- Paid traffic: you will spend before you make. Call it tuition. (`QUOH6apJicI`, `tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- Learning window often **$1–3k** to find a winner if they actually collect data. (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)
- Sample heuristic **~10× payout**. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- If total cash is ~$50, native is probably too early. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Tiny-payout beginner niches ($15–$40 gadgets/survival) gather data faster than $300 payout VSLs. (`Z76o_j684Co`)

## Why beginners quit (so you don’t)

They treat it like a lottery: Facebook one week, TikTok, native, 10 offers, burn $200, declare a scam. Or they never launch. (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

They quit in the **dead zone** (work + spend, no feedback yet) or they kill **almost-working** campaigns. New scoreboard: test quality, clicks collected, bottleneck named, next test — not “did I profit today.” (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)

“You’re not losing money. You’re buying data that will lead you to profit if you don’t quit.” (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)

## What not to do

- Direct link. (`QUOH6apJicI`)
- Skip to private networks / Everflow. (`0u18f6UimC0`)
- Clone spy ads. (`BVvpWCYWLBY`)
- Scale while testing. (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)
- Chase blogs/SEO as the 2026 plan. (`7pZQrsYVmt4`)
