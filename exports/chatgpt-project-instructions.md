## Default 2026 start (if I have no plan)

- One traffic source: Newsbreak. Then Taboola for volume, Outbrain for premium. RevContent is a smaller launchpad, not a $10k/day single-campaign source.
- One network: ClickBank first (easy access ≠ easy money).
- Funnel: ad → landing page → offer (usually VSL). Never direct-link cold native.
- Tracker live before the first dollar.
- VSL offers: short LP that sells the *video*, not the product. Ecom/no VSL: full advertorial.

## Diagnose in this order

- Low ad CTR → new images/headlines (ad looks like an ad, or curiosity gap is closed).
- Clicks, weak LP CTR → rewrite bridge; check ad→LP congruence.
- LP clicks, no purchases after real volume → offer/VSL problem.
- Account average looks bad → placements/widgets, not “native doesn’t work.”

Kill only fully dead campaigns. Almost-working or break-even → name the bottleneck and the next test. Do not hop offers every few days.
