# Landing pages

## Rule

On native, a page between the ad and the offer is **always** required. “You cannot send someone directly from an ad to a sales page and expect it to convert.” (`QUOH6apJicI`, `hsSeB7VZe88`, `98sK0XBPI4w`)

Cold native traffic needs warming. Direct linking is a “huge error.” (`-Wmdkv_dk_E`)

The LP is irrelevant if the ad never earns the click. (`eqK7yPNX5NE`)  
The LP also “doesn’t matter” until tracking and a real test are in place — don’t obsess over the perfect page while nothing is live. (`mXsnj-atKa8` — when that transcript exists)

## Which LP type

| Offer type | LP |
|---|---|
| VSL / ClickBank video | **Short page that sells the video, not the product.** (`ZW0_WjcocT8`, `hsSeB7VZe88`) |
| Ecom / no long VSL | **Full advertorial** + social proof. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`, `Ui_15Kbnj7g`) |

Funnel in one line: native ad → advertorial/pre-sell → VSL/product. Ad = angle. Advertorial = belief. VSL = the sale. (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

## Builder / speed

He uses **Convertri** (also names ClickFunnels, Leadpages). Native users bounce on slow pages — “every second of load time loses” paid clicks. (`neFBYSi5bUM`, `LAOo9CDu_Vw`, `nTUvHXg2xuY`)

Beginner LP: relevant, engaging, curiosity/desire **before** the product. Drag-and-drop is enough. (`QUOH6apJicI`)

## Killer LP pieces (`4ZMSMmVIIAg`)

1. **Headline** — curiosity + a specific benefit (not the whole secret). Example pattern: “this one weird trick burns 11 pounds…”
2. **Hero image** — test it; same copy, different images moved the campaign on a fair ~$120/variant test.
3. **Copy** — extend the headline; warm, don’t dump the offer.
4. **CTA** — large, obvious, bright (orange/green). Avoid red buttons.
5. **Alignment** — ad promise, LP, and offer must be the same person/story (no belly-fat ad → bodybuilder LP).

## Diagnose LP

Low pre-sell CTR → rewrite copy **and** check ad→LP congruence (same curiosity thread). (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

Don’t test 10 LPs at $100/day. Few pages, enough clicks each. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
