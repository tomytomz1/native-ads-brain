## How Tom picks an offer

Traffic source and offer are **one decision**. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`)

Do **not** sort ClickBank by highest commission. “75% commission on an offer with a weak VSL… is worth absolutely nothing.” (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)

**Filters (use together):**

1. **Rising gravity**, not a huge stale number. High gravity often means you are late; mid-range with an upward trend. Brand-new listings: gravity sample is too small — don’t take it literally. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `ZW0_WjcocT8`, `CEG5X39hONA`)
2. **EPC** = funnel quality. High gravity + weak EPC = affiliates sending traffic that doesn’t pay. You cannot fix a bad funnel with better traffic. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)
3. **AOV / upsells**, not just front-end commission. Strong AOV lets you outbid. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `cyynHqTOOjQ`)
4. **Watch the entire VSL** before spending. First 60s: specific curiosity. Body: story + mechanism + proof. Close: real urgency, not a reset timer. Weak VSL → skip. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `cyynHqTOOjQ`)
5. **Vendor is serious** — assets, affiliate manager, updated resource center. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)
6. **Market timing.** Evergreen (joint pain, blood sugar, cognitive) slower to peak and slower to die. Trend offers spike then compress. Know which you are buying. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`)

**Native LP is part of offer fit.** Do not send Newsbreak users straight to the VSL. VSL offers: short LP that sells the video. Ecom / no VSL: full advertorial. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`)

## Networks

| Network | Role |

| MaxBounty | Closer review; sometimes a phone call. (`FOa3UdQEF2Y`, `CFsheB1CSCk`) |

| ShareASale, Awin, CJ | Brand/ecom programs. (`Ui_15Kbnj7g`) |

**Approval script:** one traffic source, one vertical, landing page + tracking, honest “new but focused.” Do not claim every source or “I’ll run whatever converts.” Don’t sound like spam / brand bidding. Path to private networks runs through public ones. (`FOa3UdQEF2Y`, `0u18f6UimC0`)

## Niches that fit native (2026 Newsbreak / over-35 news readers)

Health/natural (joint, blood sugar, BP, cognitive, vision, energy) · survival / off-grid · financial (compliance-heavy) · DIY / self-reliance. **Not** teen gadgets or gaming on Newsbreak. (`ZW0_WjcocT8`, `cyynHqTOOjQ`)

**If budget is small / beginner “easy” path:** skip $300 payout offers. Prefer $15–$40 physical gadgets, survival digital, comfort/posture, home problem-solvers, low-payout mindset offers — faster data, less supplement compliance. (`Z76o_j684Co`)

**Seasonal:** Jan / Q1 health + weight + preparedness. Q4 survival / energy / gifts. (`Rl5Bm5SLtsY`, `JCCfHx55Wtg`)

## Spy tools

He uses **Anstrex**. Filter CB / BuyGoods / MaxBounty. Signal: ~10–14 days running. Do **not** clone images (platforms penalize duplicate content). Steal *patterns* (angle, hook, advertorial type), not the ad. (`BVvpWCYWLBY`, `vPe-T4-ZYvU`)
