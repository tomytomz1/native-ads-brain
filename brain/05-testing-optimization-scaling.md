# Testing, optimization, scaling

## Before the first dollar

Congruent funnel: ad → LP → offer. Tracking live. Strong offer **and** ad/LP, not “just the affiliate link.” (`APOXFiDALRQ`, `QUOH6apJicI`)

## Sample size and moving parts

- ~**10× payout** to collect a first sample (ads + site placements). (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Daily budget must match how many variants you run. $100/day with 20 ads × 10 LPs = 200 combos = wash of data. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Keep **5–7 creatives** live. Same rule at $100/day and $10k/day. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- Be frugal on daily spend even if the bankroll is large — patterns show at $100/day that also show at $1k/day. Fail fast if it’s dead; don’t fail slow by overspending a bad test. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)
- **$50/day structure:** one proven offer, three angles, two creatives + three headlines per angle, split budget evenly so one ad doesn’t hog spend. Goal is a CTR/conversion *signal*, not day-1 profit. Don’t test one idea at a time on a small budget. (`4ij3KJvH15c`) Still keep **5–7 creatives live** on the native algo — prepare the set, don’t dump 18 ads into one campaign. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)

## Diagnose (in order)

| Signal | Do this |
|---|---|
| Low ad CTR | New images/headlines. Creative looks like an ad. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `tIoJ1Tg0AsA`, `neFBYSi5bUM`) |
| Clicks, weak LP CTR | Rewrite bridge; check ad→LP congruence. (`neFBYSi5bUM`) |
| LP clicks, no purchases after real volume | VSL/offer problem — don’t keep buying the same funnel. (`vPe-T4-ZYvU`, `neFBYSi5bUM`) |
| Account average looks bad | Check **placements / widgets**. Kill junk, keep winners. Confirm with a small retest before scaling. (`98sK0XBPI4w`, `U8v_7KDMbNo`) |

Metrics: ad CTR, pre-sell CTR, conversion rate. (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

## Kill vs continue

- **Fully dead** → kill, next offer. (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- **Almost working** (up day, down day, spike, nothing) → usually one piece away. Resetting is the expensive habit. (`tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- **Break-even** → optimize, don’t pull the plug. (`kxAziYkXBds`)
- Don’t quit in the data-collection window. First campaign probably won’t be profitable. (`QUOH6apJicI`, `tIoJ1Tg0AsA`)
- Don’t hop offers every couple of days. (`7ax4Qjq9Lx4`)

## Optimize before you scale

Move spend onto winning ads, LPs, and placements **at low budget**. Scaling exaggerates whatever ROI you already have. He’s seen $thousands/day at 10% ROI — cut spend, optimize, then rescale. (`APOXFiDALRQ`)

Always keep a little testing against the control; ads fatigue. (`neFBYSi5bUM`)

## Scale

- **20–30% budget every 48 hours** on Newsbreak and “most other native platforms.” Not doubling. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `98sK0XBPI4w`)
- Roadmap video also says **~40% every 48 hours** once optimized. (`neFBYSi5bUM`) Prefer the slower step unless they already have clean ROI.
- Then: video of the winning image angle (Newsbreak). Then Taboola for volume / Outbrain for premium. (`hsSeB7VZe88`, `0pz9qjlt5BU`)
- At $5–10k/day: more angles, tighter tracking, placement-sensitive media buying. Funnel is still ad → lander → offer. (`0UFS23M2W1I`)
- $70k days are peaks (right offer × angle × source), not a daily baseline. (`2aM17rY3icg`)

## Spy

Longevity ~10–14 days ≈ probably profitable. Don’t copy the ad. (`BVvpWCYWLBY`)
